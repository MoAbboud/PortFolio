# tektak - Overview

Public document. Behaviour only.

## What this is

tektak is a text-only summary of what is happening on TikTok. A curator writes short
write-ups of the drama worth knowing about, and readers get the substance without opening
the app and losing an hour.

There is no video, no feed, no autoplay, and nothing to scroll into. That is the product,
not a limitation of it.

## The problem it addresses

People want to know what everyone is talking about without being on the platform where
everyone is talking about it. Opening TikTok to catch up costs an hour and delivers three
minutes of information. The existing alternatives are the same feed with a different logo.

## What it does

There are two sides, used by two different people, or by one person at two different times.

### For the reader

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Read the latest | The current stories, each with a title, a short summary and a category |
| See what is trending | The hashtags currently worth knowing, with rough scale |
| Know the sources | The accounts that break or spread the stories, and what each is known for |
| Finish | The page ends. There is nothing below the bottom |

### For the curator

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Add a story | A title, a two or three sentence summary, a category, related hashtags, a source account and rough engagement numbers |
| Add a hashtag | A name, a category and rough scale |
| Add a source account | A handle, a follower count, whether it is verified, and what it is known for |
| Review and remove | See everything currently published and delete individual entries |
| Start over | Clear everything |

## Categories

Every story is filed under one of: drama, scandal, feud, controversy, response, or legal.
The list is fixed. A story that does not fit one of them is a story this site is not about.

## How to use it

### As a reader

Open the site. Read. Leave.

### As a curator

1. Open the curation page.
2. Spend ten to fifteen minutes going through wherever the drama actually surfaces.
3. Add two or three stories. Keep the summary to two or three sentences.
4. Refresh the reader page to see the result.
5. Update the trending hashtags weekly and the featured accounts monthly.

## What it does not do

- It does not embed, host, or link to video.
- It does not pull anything automatically. Every word on the site was typed by a person.
- It does not have accounts, logins, comments or reactions.
- It does not have an infinite feed, a recommendation engine, or notifications.
- It does not verify anything. The stories are one curator's summary of what is being said.
- It does not sync between devices. What one browser holds, another does not.

## Requirements to run it

A browser. There is no server, no account and no installation. The curation page is a
separate page and should not be linked from the reader page, because anyone who can open it
can change what is published.
