# whereyago - Overview

Public document. Behaviour only.

## What this is

whereyago is a day logger for people who are bad at planning days out. Someone who had a
good day records it stop by stop. Someone who cannot decide what to do browses those real
days and copies one.

The unit of content is a whole day, not a single place. A restaurant listing tells you
where to eat. A logged day tells you what to do from ten in the morning until eleven at
night, in an order that already worked for somebody.

## The problem it addresses

Planning a day out means stitching together four or five separate decisions - where to
start, how far apart things are, what fits the weather, where to eat in between - and most
recommendation apps only answer one of those at a time. People end up doing the same three
things repeatedly, or nothing.

## What it does

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Log a day | Record a day as an ordered list of stops, each with a name, a kind, a rough time and an optional note |
| Tag a vibe | Label the whole day as chill, foodie, family, adventure, night, culture or outdoors |
| See it on a map | Stops are placed on a map in order, so the shape of the day is visible before committing to it |
| Attach conditions | The weather the day was actually had in, so a reader knows whether it transfers to a rainy Saturday |
| Browse Discover | A public feed of days other people shared, filterable by vibe |
| Recreate a day | Open any stop in a maps app and get directions, one tap per stop |
| Keep a profile | An account, a personal list of logged days, and the days that are shared publicly |

## How to use it

1. Create an account with an email, a username and a password.
2. Tap the centre button to log a day. Give it a title, pick a vibe, add the city and date.
3. Add stops in the order they happened. Each stop takes a name, a type and optionally a
   time and a note.
4. Save. The day appears in your profile.
5. To find something to do, open Home and switch to the map or list view, or open Discover
   and filter by the vibe you are in the mood for.
6. Open a day, read its stops, and use the directions link on each stop to follow it.

## What it does not do

- It does not book anything. No reservations, no tickets, no payments.
- It does not invent itineraries. Every day in the feed is a day a person actually had.
- It does not rank or score places. It shows days, and days are only as good as the person
  who logged them.
- It does not do live location tracking or check-ins.
- It does not currently support likes, comments, ratings or following, although the app is
  designed with room for them.

## Requirements to run it

A phone running the mobile app, an internet connection, and an account. There is also a
browser prototype that runs from a single file with no account and no server, kept as a
design reference rather than a product.
