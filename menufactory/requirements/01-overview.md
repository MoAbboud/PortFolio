# Menu Factory - Overview

Public document. Behaviour only. "Menu Factory" is a working name and will change.

## What this is

**Win your customers over before they even show up.**

A library of restaurant and bar websites built to impress: full designs with motion,
digital art and atmosphere, made to feel like walking into the place. A restaurant browses
the library, picks the look that fits who they are, and gets a site on its own domain with
its own menu, photos and logo in it - plus a simple admin page where the owner keeps the
content current without touching the design.

The point is identity. Most small restaurants have a plain site that looks like every other
restaurant on the same builder. Menu Factory offers them a designed, elevated look without
an agency's fee - and, on the monthly plan, the freedom to change it. Tired of your basic
site? Switch up the look.

## The problem it exists for

A restaurant puts years into its aesthetic: the room, the lighting, the glassware, the menu
card. Then its website is a template from a site builder, the same layout as the place down
the street. A guest deciding where to go tonight gets no sense of the place at all.

The choices on offer are a site builder they pay every month and still have to design
themselves, the free page that comes with their payment system, or an agency that charges
thousands for a custom site that is then frozen for years.

## What a restaurant gets

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| A designed look | A full design made for a kind of place - motion, digital art, type, layout - with the restaurant's own logo and photos |
| The library | Every look can be browsed, and the restaurant can see its own menu in each one before choosing |
| Switch the look | On the monthly plan, the restaurant can ask to move to another look in the library whenever it wants a change, and the switch is made once it is approved. Nothing is re-entered |
| Less motion, more motion | A button on every page: the full show, or a calmer version of the same design. It starts from the guest's own device setting and remembers their choice |
| Owner admin | A simple page where the owner changes the menu, prices, hours, happy hour, events, notices and photos, previews the change and publishes it. The design itself is not editable there, so it cannot be broken |
| A real menu | Every dish and drink as text: name, description, ingredients, price, and marks such as vegetarian, spicy or zero-proof |
| Open now and happy hour | Whether the place is open right now, and whether it is happy hour, in its own time zone, including late nights and holidays |
| Events and notices | Live music, DJ nights, specials and closures, each with dates, gone on their own when the date passes |
| Find and call | Address with directions in the guest's own maps app, a phone number that dials when tapped |
| Its own domain | The restaurant owns its domain name, in its own name |

Planned for later: the restaurant's Google reviews on the site, with the owner choosing which
quotes to show, and a view in the admin of how each menu item is talked about online - good,
mixed or bad.

## The library

Looks are grouped by category - cocktail bars, bars, restaurants, and more as the library
grows. The first to be built:

1. Cocktail bars
2. Mexican restaurants
3. American restaurants

Each look is shown in the library with a made-up venue. A restaurant is not limited to its
own kind; any look works with any menu.

## What it does not do

- Online ordering, delivery or reservations. A Menu Factory site is the restaurant's own
  site, not a front for an ordering service.
- Take payments of any kind.
- Collect guests' personal details. No sign-ups, no tracking cookies.
- Let the owner edit the design. Owners control their content; the art stays as designed.
