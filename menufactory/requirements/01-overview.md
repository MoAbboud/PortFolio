# resto-dir - Overview

Public document. Behaviour only.

## What this is

A set of website templates made for restaurants, and the service of setting one up. A
restaurant picks a look, hands over its menu, hours, address and photos, and gets a fast,
finished website on its own domain - without learning a site builder or paying one every
month for as long as the site exists.

Every template is built for the person who actually uses a restaurant website: someone on a
phone, often standing outside or deciding where to eat in the next ten minutes, who wants
four things - is it open, what is on the menu, where is it, and how do I call, book or order.

## The problem it exists for

Most small restaurants end up with one of three websites:

- A site-builder site the owner set up once, paid for every month since, and has not
  touched. The hours are last year's and the menu is a photo of the printed one.
- A PDF menu linked from a page. It is unreadable on a phone, search engines cannot read the
  dishes, and changing a price means making a new PDF.
- The free page that comes with their point-of-sale or ordering system. It works, and it
  looks like every other restaurant on the same system.

What a restaurant needs is simpler than what a site builder sells: a handful of pages that
load instantly, a real menu, hours that are right, and someone to call when they change.

## What a restaurant gets

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| A real menu | Every dish as text: name, description, price, and dietary marks such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or spicy. Readable on a phone, searchable, printable. Several menus where a restaurant has them - lunch, dinner, drinks, brunch |
| Open now | The site says whether the restaurant is open right now and when it next opens or closes, in the restaurant's own time zone, including holiday hours |
| Find and call | Address with a map and directions, a phone number that dials when tapped |
| Order, book, deliver | Buttons that go straight to the ordering, reservation and delivery services the restaurant already uses. The site never takes payment itself |
| Its own look | A template chosen to suit the place, in the restaurant's colours, fonts and logo, with its own photos |
| Fast everywhere | Pages are plain files on a global host. They open quickly on a weak phone signal |
| Found by search | Hours, address, cuisine and menu are written in the form search engines and maps read, so the details they show match the site |
| Usable by everyone | Built to the WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines: readable contrast, works with a keyboard and a screen reader, text that scales |
| Announcements | A short notice at the top - closed for a holiday, a new seasonal menu, a private event - with an end date so it takes itself down |
| Its own domain | The restaurant owns its domain name, in its own name. If it ever leaves, the site and the domain go with it |

## The templates

Each template is a complete design for a kind of restaurant, not a colour scheme. All of them
read the same restaurant details, so a restaurant can see its own menu in every template
before choosing, and can change its mind later without re-entering anything.

The first set planned:

| Template | For |
| --- | --- |
| Counter | Casual and quick: barbecue, tacos, burgers, diners, food trucks. The menu and the order button come first |
| Table | Sit-down and evening: bistros, steakhouses, wine bars. Atmosphere, reservations and a calmer menu |
| Morning | Cafes, bakeries and brunch spots. Early hours, the pastry case, the coffee list |

Each one is shown on the showcase page filled in with a made-up restaurant, so it can be
tried without anyone's real details.

## What it does not do

- Take orders or payments. Restaurants already have a system for that; the site points to it.
- Collect diners' personal details. No sign-ups, no loyalty accounts, no tracking cookies.
- Replace the restaurant's point-of-sale, reservation book or delivery apps.
- Write the menu. The restaurant's own words go on its site.
